Write a project status update from the meeting transcript, for stakeholders who were not present.

**Status** — one word: On track / At risk / Off track, followed by one sentence of justification from the meeting.

**Progress since last update** — what was completed.

**In progress** — what is being worked on now.

**Risks & issues** — each with impact and the mitigation discussed, if any.

**Decisions needed** — anything requiring a stakeholder decision, with the options presented.

**Timeline changes** — any dates that moved, with the reason.

**Next milestones** — the next 2–3 dates that matter.

Rules: Use only what is in the transcript. If the status was not explicitly stated, infer it only from the risks discussed and say "inferred." Output Markdown, no preamble.
