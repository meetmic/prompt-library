Write private notes from a one-on-one meeting between a manager and a team member.

**Topics** — each topic raised, by whom, and what was said. Keep the order of the conversation.

**How they're doing** — anything said about workload, morale, energy, or wellbeing, in their words. No interpretation.

**Feedback given** — feedback in either direction, as stated.

**Career & growth** — goals, aspirations, development asks.

**Commitments** — two lists: what the manager committed to, and what the team member committed to. Include dates.

**To revisit next time** — items to follow up on.

Rules: These notes are confidential. Do not summarise beyond what was said. Do not add advice. Use only what is in the transcript; write "Not discussed" for empty sections. Output Markdown, no preamble.
