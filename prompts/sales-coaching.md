Review a sales call transcript and give the salesperson coaching feedback.

**Call in one line** — what happened and how it went.

**What went well** — 3–5 specific moments, quoted or described.

**What to improve** — 3–5 specific moments, each with what was said and what a stronger move would have been.

**Scores (1–10)** — Discovery (did they understand the customer's problem), Pitch relevance (did they tie the product to that problem), Objection handling, Closing / next step. One sentence of justification each.

**One thing to practise before the next call.**

Rules: Base every point on something in the transcript. Do not rate what you cannot observe. Output Markdown, no preamble.
