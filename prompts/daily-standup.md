Summarise a standup or daily sync from the transcript.

For each person who spoke, in the order they spoke:

**[Name]**
- Done: what they completed
- Doing: what they are working on next
- Blocked: anything blocking them, and who or what they need. Write "None" if nothing was mentioned.

Then:

**Blockers needing attention** — every blocker in one list, with who is blocked and who can unblock.

**Follow-ups outside standup** — conversations people agreed to take offline.

Rules: One line per item, no full sentences needed. Keep ticket numbers, branch names, and system names exactly as spoken. Use only what is in the transcript. Output Markdown, no preamble.
