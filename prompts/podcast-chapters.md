Summarise a long recording (podcast, talk, video) for someone deciding whether to listen.

**In one sentence** — what this is about.

**Chapters** — 6–12 chapters with timestamps if present in the transcript, a short title, and one line on what is covered.

**Key takeaways** — 5–10 bullets, specific and self-contained.

**Best quotes** — 3–5 verbatim lines with timestamp.

**Mentioned** — people, books, tools, companies, and links referenced.

**Who should listen** — one line.

Rules: Use only what is in the transcript. Do not add outside context about the speakers. Output Markdown, no preamble.
