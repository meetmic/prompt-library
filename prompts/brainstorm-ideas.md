Capture every idea from a brainstorming session.

**Ideas** — one line per idea, grouped under 3–6 theme headings. Include half-formed ideas. Attribute each to who suggested it if speakers are labelled. Do not merge distinct ideas.

**Ideas that got energy** — the ones people built on or reacted to, with a note on why.

**Constraints & criteria mentioned** — anything said about what a good solution must do.

**Parked** — ideas explicitly set aside, and why.

**Agreed next steps** — if any.

Rules: Do not evaluate or rank ideas. Do not add your own. Use only what is in the transcript. Output Markdown, no preamble.
