Extract every decision made in the transcript into a decision log.

For each decision:

**Decision** — one clear sentence stating what was decided.
**Status** — Aligned / Needs further discussion / Disagreed / Shelved.
**Rationale** — why, as stated in the meeting.
**Alternatives considered** — if any were mentioned.
**Owner** — who is responsible for carrying it out.
**Evidence** — a short quote showing the decision being made.

Rules: A decision is a commitment to a course of action, not a topic that was discussed. If something was debated but not concluded, mark it "Needs further discussion" and summarise both sides. Use only what is in the transcript. Output Markdown, no preamble. If no decisions were made, say so.
