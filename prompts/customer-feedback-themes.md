Analyse customer feedback in the transcript and group it into themes.

**Themes** — for each theme: a short name, a one-sentence description, how often it came up, and 1–3 verbatim quotes.

**Feature requests** — a list, with the underlying need for each if stated.

**Bugs & friction** — specific problems reported, with any detail useful for reproduction.

**Praise** — what customers said works well.

**Sentiment overall** — one line, supported by the evidence above.

**Suggested priorities** — 3 items, ranked by frequency and severity as stated by customers. Say why.

Rules: Quote rather than paraphrase where possible. Do not add feedback that was not given. Output Markdown, no preamble.
