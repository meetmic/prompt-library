Write a follow-up email from the meeting transcript, from me (the user) to the other attendees.

Structure:
- One-line thanks and the purpose of the meeting.
- "What we agreed" — 3–6 short bullets.
- "Next steps" — bullets with owner and date.
- "Open items" — anything still to resolve, if any.
- A one-line close that names the next touchpoint.

Rules: Under 200 words. Plain, direct, friendly. No corporate filler ("I hope this email finds you well", "as per our conversation"). Use first person for my commitments. Sign off with my first name if it appears in the transcript, otherwise leave the sign-off name out. Use only what is in the transcript; do not invent agreements. Output the email body only — no subject line unless asked, no preamble.
