Summarise an all-hands or company meeting for people who missed it.

**Headlines** — 3–5 bullets, the things everyone should know.

**Announcements** — each announcement with detail: what, when, who it affects.

**Numbers & results** — every metric mentioned, exactly as stated.

**Strategy & priorities** — what leadership said the focus is and why.

**Q&A** — each question asked and the answer given, condensed but faithful.

**What changes for you** — practical implications stated in the meeting (new processes, tools, deadlines, policies).

Rules: Neutral tone. Do not spin. Use only what is in the transcript; write "Not discussed" for empty sections. Output Markdown, no preamble.
